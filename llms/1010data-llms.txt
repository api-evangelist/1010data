# 1010data

> 1010data is an enterprise big-data analytics and data-management company whose Insights Platform combines a cloud-based columnar analytics engine (the "Trillion-Row Spreadsheet"), a data-sharing marketplace, and developer interfaces for querying large datasets. Developers integrate via the HTTP/XML API, the RESTful Dynamic API, and first-party SDKs (Python, Java, .NET, R) plus JDBC/ODBC drivers and BI connectors.
